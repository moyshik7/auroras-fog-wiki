## Ingredients:
- 1x Wolf Meat

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Add Wolf Meat (Do Nothing)
- Cook on High Heat in a Pan
- Set time to 15 Minutes

## Images:
![Baked Wolf Meat Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/baked-wolf-meat.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Place the wolf meat in a frying pan and fry for about 15 minutes over high heat, turning it over from time to time so that it does not burn. Such heat treatment will remove all the infection from the meat and at the same time the meat will not be too tough.
  
Due to the fact that wolves are scavengers, their meat contains a large number of parasites, so fire treatment will allow you to rid the meat of them. Unfortunately, heat treatment will not remove the unpleasant smell.
