## Ingredients:
- 3x [Chamomile Flowers](/herbs/chamomile/) or
- 3x Valerian Flowers (optional)
- 3x Motherwort Flowers (optional)
- Water

## Where to craft?
- The stove at Shaartan's house
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price

## Recipe:
- Add Water (Boiled)
- Add 3 Chamomile Flowers (Do Nothing)
- Add 3 Valerian FLowers (Do Nothing) (Optional)
- Add 3 Motherwort FLowers (Do Nothing) (Optional)
- Set Heat to Low
- Cook in a Kettle
- Set Time for 15 minutes

## Images:
![Chamomile Decoction Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/chamomile-decoction.png)


## In Game Instructions:
Pour half a liter to the kettle and bring it to boil. All collected flowers add to the settle, and then simmer the contents for about 15 minutes in a water bath, over low heat. Then remove from the heat and wrap for a couple of hours. Take a few minutes before bedtime in a warm form.
  
This set of herbs should improve the quality of sleep and reduce fatigue. In the absence of valerian or motherwort, you can make a decoction only from chamomile, but this will weaken the effect of medicinal properties.
