## Ingredients:
- 5x Turnips
- 1x Carrot
- 1x Onion
- Water

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Add Water (Boiled)
- Add 5x Turnips (Chopped)
- Add 1x Carrot (Chopped)
- Add 1x Onion (Chopped)
- Cook on Low Heat in a Pot
- Set time to 10 Minutes

![Vegetable Soup Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/vegetable-soup.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Add half a liter of water in a cooking pot and bring to a boil, In a cooking pot with boiling water, add the vegetables cut into large slices.
  
Cook the soup until the vegetables become soft, usually about 10 minutes is enough. If you digest the soup, the vegetables will lose their useful properties, and the broth will be bitter.
  
It is better to cook the stew over low heat, to preserve its beneficial properties, and covering the dish with a lid.
