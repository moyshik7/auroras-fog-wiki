## Ingredients:
- 1x Pennybun
- 3x Seaweed
- 1x Onion
- 1x Carp
- 3x Turnip
- 1x Carrot

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Select Pot from "Craft Tools"
- Add half liter water and boil
- Clean and cut carp
- Chop Seaweed
- Chop Pennybun
- Cook them for awhile
- Add Turnip (chopped), Carrots (chopped), onions (chopped)
- Cook on medium heat
- Set cooking duration to 15 minutes
