## Ingredients:
- 1x Pennybun
- 3x Seaweed
- 1x Onion
- 1x Carp
- 3x Turnip
- 1x Carrot
- Water

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Select Pot from "Craft Tools"
- Add half liter (1) water and boil
- Add Carp (Cleaned)
- Add Seaweed (Chopped)
- Add Pennybun (Chopped)
- Add Turnip (Chopped)
- Add Carrots (Chopped)
- Add Onions (Chopped)
- Cook on medium heat
- Set cooking duration to 15 minutes

## Game Instructions:
In a pot or cast iron, add half a liter of water and bring to a boil. Then cut cleaned carp into several pieces, and add it to boiling water. Add chopped seaweed and finely chopped pennybun to the broth and let them cook a little and give the juice to the broth. After just a couple of minutes, add the remaining ingredients: turnips, carrots and onions, prematurely peeling them and finely slicing them.
  
The duration of cooking seaweed soup depends on the type of fish, about 15 minutes will be enough for carp. Digestion can deprive seaweed and vegetables of most of the nutrients, as well as worsening the taste of fish, making it tough, and the taste of broth bittery.
