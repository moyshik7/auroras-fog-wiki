## Ingredients:
- 3x Chicory Root
- Water

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Add 3x Chicory Root (Crushed)
- Add 1x Water (Boiled)
- Select Pot
- Set time to 5 Minutes
- Set heat to low

## Images:
![Chicory Tea Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/chicory-tea.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Take the roots of chicory and grind them in a mortar to a powder state. Put the crushed roots in a cast iron or clay pot and pour cold water. About half a liter will be enough. The water must be boiled, and then cook the chicory on low heat for about 5 minutes.
  
Chicory tea perfectly helps to restore strength and recharge your batteries.
