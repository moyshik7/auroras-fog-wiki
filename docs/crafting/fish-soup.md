## Ingredients:
- 1x Carp
- 3x Turnip
- 1x Carrot
- 2x Onion
- Water

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Select Pot from "Craft Tools"
- Add half liter water and boil
- Add Carp (Cleaned)
- Add Turnips (Chopped)
- Add Carrots (Chopped)
- Add Onions (Chopped)
- Set Heat to Low
- Set duration to 15 minutes

![Fish Soup Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/fish-soup.png)


## In Game Instructions:
Add half a liter of water in a cooking pot and bring to a boil. Clean the crucian and add it to boiling water. Take turnips, carrots and necessarily onions and cut into large slices, and then add to the pot.
  
The duration of cooking fish soup depends on the type of fish, about 15 minutes will be enough for crucian carp. Digestion can greatly worsen the quality of fish meat, making it tough and will worsen the taste of the broth.
Fish soup will turn out much tastier if you cook it without a lid, in an open dish and on low heat.
