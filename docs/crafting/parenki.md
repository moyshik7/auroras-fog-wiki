## Ingredients:
- 5x Carrots
- Water

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Add 5x Carrots (Chopped)
- Add Water (Boiled)
- Cook on Medium Heat in a Pot
- Set time to 20 Minutes

## Images:
![Parenki Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/parenki.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Carrots should be cut into large pieces and put in a cast iron or clay pot. Then pour a small amount of water, about half a liter will be enough, and bring this water to a boil. After boiling the water, you need to steam the sliced pieces until they are half ready, and then pull them out and let them brew a little.
  
It is worth steaming parenki for no more than 20 minutes over medium heat so that they are not too overcooked and so that the carrots does not lose its useful properties.
  
Parenki can also be prepared from other root vegetables: pumpkin, beets, turnips. Only in this case, vegetables should be taken in larger quantities.
