## Ingredients:
- 5x Saffron Milk Cup
- 2x Onion

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Add 5x Saffron Milk Cup Mushroom (Chopped)
- Add 2x Onions
- Cook on Medium Heat in a Pan
- Set time to 20 Minutes

## Images:
![Fried Saffron Milk Cup Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/fried-saffron-milk-cup.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Saffron milk cap need to be divided into several parts: large ones into 8 parts, small ones into 4 parts, and very small ones can be fried whole. After that, pour the chopped saffrons into a frying pan and fry over medium heat until all the moisture evaporates from the saffrons. At the end of evaporation, add finely chopped onion to the saffrons. Onion will reduce the slight bitterness of saffrons, and will also serve as a seasoning.
  
After adding the onions, the dish should be cooked over medium heat for about 20 minutes without covering with a lid. At the same time, the dish must be constantly stirred so that the heat treatment is uniform.
