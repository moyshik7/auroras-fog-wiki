## Ingredients:
- 3x Nettle Leaves
- 5x Turnips
- 1x Carrot
- 1x Onion
- Water

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Add Water (Boiled)
- Add 3x Nettle Leaves (Chopped)
- Add 5x Turnips (Chopped)
- Add 1x Carrot (Chopped)
- Add 1x Onion (Chopped)
- Cook on Low Heat in a Pot
- Set time to 15 Minutes

![Nettle Soup Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/nettle-soup.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Add a liter of water to the cooking pot and bring it to boil. Place finely chopped nettles in boiling water. Then take turnios, carrots, onions and cut into small slices, and then add to the broth.
  
After adding all the vegetables, let the broth cook for about 15 minutes on low heat, stirring only occasionally. During this time, the nettle will give its juice and a unique smell, but if you digest our soup, the nettle will lose most of its useful properties.
  
Due to the content of a large number of useful properties in nettle, this soup allows you to quickly recover from diseases and significantly increases Immunity.
