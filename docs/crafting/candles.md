## Ingredients:
- 3x Animal Fat
- 1x Thread

## Where to craft?
- Any stove available to use
    - Stoves can be used at NPCs houses for a price
    - After buying your own house you can use your stove for free

## Recipe:
- Heat Up 3x Animal Fat
- Add a Thread
- Set time to 40 minutes
- Set Temperature to High

## Images:
![Candle Recipe in Aurora's Fog](/img/crafting/candle.webp)


## In Game Instructions:
Place animal fat in a cooking pot and heat stirring until it takes a homogeneous mass. After that, the mass must be poured into a mold and when it begins to solidify, place a thread in it as a wick.
  
Due to the large amount of fat, kindling can take up to 40 minutes, so it is better to melt it over high. heat, stirring the mass from time to time.
  
The finished candle can be used both in the household and for replacement in portable lanterns.
