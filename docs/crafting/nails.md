## Ingredients:
- 2 x Iron Ore (Gray)

## Where to craft?
- Blasksmithing station at Yorta's House



## Recipe:
- Fill B3, C3 and D3 of the crafting grid with  Iron Ore (Gray Ore)

### Blasksmith grid layout naming reference:

| A1 | A2 | A3 | A4 | A5 |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 | B5 |
| C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 |
| D1 | D2 | D3 | D4 | D5 |
| E1 | E2 | E3 | E4 | E5 |
