## Added
- Added ripple effect on Yotra's body during the act.
- Added the possibility of manul penetration at the beginning of the act.
- Added the ability to change the penetration depth.
- Added the possibility of an alternative penetration type.
- Added the ability to change the depth of alternative penetration.
- Added the ability to change the penetration type during the act.
- Added the ability to finish outside.
- Breathing animation has been added to all characters. Act's speed and the type of penetration affect the intensity of breathing. 
- Breathing sounds have also been added, which are also affected (volume and speed change) by the intensity parameter of the act.
- The speed and frequency of the partner's blinking now varies depending on the satisfaction parameter.
- Added new moans for Yotra. They are now divided into several categories: weak and strong. They are played depending on the satisfaction of the partner. This parameter also affects the frequency of moans and their volume.
- Added animations for Yotra's upper body. They unlocked as you level up the position.
- Added orgasm animations for Yotra: normal and strong. They are played depending on whether it was the first time in the act or not.
- Added kiss animations. A total of 4 animations were made; they depend on the position of the Yotra's upper body and the type of penetration.
- Hero's head is now shown in a third-person mode.
- Added the ability to change the position of the hero's hands during the act.
