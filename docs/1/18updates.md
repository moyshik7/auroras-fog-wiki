## Versions
- ### The Early Access Era (v0.06 - v0.08)
    - v0.06.1 (March 2022): Focused on audio (breath sounds), lighting (night brightness), and bug fixes (Nami's eye glitch).   
    - v0.07 (Sept 2022): The Physics Update. Major overhaul of combat, introduction of headshots, belt physics, and the frying pan.
    - v0.08 (Nov 2022): Added the Weather System.
- ### The Expansion Era (v0.09 - v0.10)
    - v0.09 (March 2023): Territory Update. Expanded world boundaries, added Coal and Dead Wood nodes.
    - v0.09.5 (Jan 2024): Crafting Update. Added automated firewood, water jugs, repair items, and blacksmith skill visualization.
- ### The Modern Era (v0.11 - v0.12)
    - v0.11 (March 2025): Added Character Vlada, Portuguese language support, and 3 new quests.
    - v0.12 (July 2025): Visuals Update. Full map rendering, new flora, updated resource locations.
    - v0.12.1 (Sept 2025): Reworked Yotra's animations.
