# Shaartan
![Shaartan in her yard](/img/npc/shaartan.jpg "Shaartan in her yard")

## Overview
Shaartan is an herbalist NPC . She offers small local quests that generally involve collecting flowers, herbs, and ingredients.  

## Location & schedule
- Typical spawn: idk
- If Shaartan isn't visible in her yard plucking herbs, look for her inside her house.

## Appearance & personality
- Appearance: practical herbalist attire, carrying satchels and a small bound journal.
- Personality: calm, methodical, and quietly curious about rare plants. She appreciates precision and favors players who bring exact requested items.

Interactions
- First meeting: greet her at the stall to unlock basic dialogue and her initial small quests.
- Repeatable quests: many of her tasks are repeatable daily or after a cooldown.
- Relationship: completing her requests increases affinity, unlocking improved recipes or discounted items.

Quests

1) Gathering Wildflowers
- Type: fetch, repeatable
- Trigger: Speak to Shaartan after the first market visit; occasionally offered automatically.
- Objective: Collect 5× Common Wildflowers (found in meadows and near rivers).
- Rewards: small herb bundle, minor XP, +affinity.
- Tips: Wildflowers respawn near river banks; bring a small stack of empty slots to avoid inventory clutter.

2) Herb Delivery
- Type: delivery
- Trigger: Unlocked after completing 2 gathering quests and raising initial affinity.
- Objective: Find 3× Blueleaf Herb (slightly rarer, found on cliff edges) and deliver them to Shaartan.
- Rewards: crafted tincture recipe, medium XP, coins.
- Tips: Blueleaf spawns are more common early morning on higher terrain. Use the map waypoint to mark likely spawn regions.

3) Tea Blend Request
- Type: collect + choice
- Trigger: High affinity or completing Herb Delivery.
- Objective: Bring one of several optional ingredients (e.g., Honey, Chamomile, Mint) — your choice influences the reward.
- Rewards: choice-based — favors, different tinctures, or a small unique herb.
- Tips: Choose the ingredient that best complements your current crafting goals.

Rewards & progression
- Rewards scale from small crafting materials and XP to unique recipes and higher affinity once you complete quest chains.
- Completing her chain often unlocks better potion recipes or discounts at her stall.

Quick tips
- Keep space in inventory for quest items; some flowers stack while others do not.
- If you miss her in town, search the caravan area east of the market.
- Save before attempting rare-ingredient quests if you rely on a single spawn.
